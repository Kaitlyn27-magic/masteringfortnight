STEP BY STEP

🎮

1. Download Fortnite

Visit the official Epic Games website and install the Epic Games Launcher. After installing it, create your Epic Games account and search for Fortnite. Press install and wait for the game to finish downloading before launching it.

🏆

2. Learn How To Play

Fortnite is a battle royale game where players collect weapons, build structures, and survive against opponents. Stay inside the storm circle, collect shields, and practice building walls and ramps to improve your survival skills.
